## A blown tyre, and Ichi Coffee

All good, though it was getting pretty dark. Then I noticed a recurring sound coming from my bike, you can actually hear it here, earlier on.

![A dark road lit only by the bike's headlight, filmed while riding at night](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/night-ride-before-the-tyre-noise-worsened.mp4 "You can actually hear it here, earlier on.")

I thought nothing of it, but it kept getting louder and worse. It sounded like someone running beside me. Certainly nothing good.

![A dark, close-up, blurred shot of the front wheel and fork at night, filmed while investigating a strange noise](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/closeup-of-noisy-tyre-at-night.mp4 "Certainly nothing good.")

Will need some research tonight, after I get some food.

So, what was it? After some research it turned out to be a "tyre blister": the layers of the tyre had delaminated, trapping air between the tread and the casing, which is what made the running sound as it went spongy. It's somewhat hard to see in the photo, but for such a new-looking tyre it unfortunately had to go straight in the bin.

![A close-up of a bicycle tyre's tread and branding, showing the delaminated blister damage](./_assets/tyre-sidewall-showing-blister-damage.jpg "A tyre blister.")

![A hand dropping the damaged tyre into a bin](./_assets/damaged-tyre-thrown-in-a-bin.jpg "Straight in the bin.")

However! I did bring a spare tyre, as the well-prepared person I am, so on the bike it went. Shame it doesn't look quite as nice.

![The loaded touring bike leaning against a wall indoors after fitting the replacement tyre](./_assets/bike-with-replacement-tyre-fitted.jpg "Shame it doesn't look quite as nice.")

Onwards. After some more classic views of cherry blossoms on rivers:

![Cherry blossom trees in bloom along a straight canal, seen from a cycle path with a metal railing](./_assets/cherry-blossom-trees-along-a-canal.jpg "More classic views of cherry blossoms on rivers.")

I reached another planned stop: a really, _really_ long wooden bridge.

![A wide, calm estuary beside a straight coastal road under an overcast sky, with a van parked at the roadside](./_assets/calm-estuary-beside-coastal-road.jpg "A really, really long wooden bridge.")

![A very long straight wooden pedestrian bridge stretching across tidal flats toward distant hills, filmed while approaching it](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/long-wooden-bridge-over-mudflats.mp4 "A really, really long wooden bridge.")

Pretty cool, and it dropped me off on a lovely road beside fields of green tea.

![A long wooden pedestrian bridge over tidal flats, framed by a blossoming cherry tree branch in the foreground, with two people walking along it](./_assets/wooden-bridge-framed-by-cherry-blossom.jpg "A really, really long wooden bridge.")

![A cycle path running beside neatly trimmed rows of green tea bushes, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/cycle-path-beside-green-tea-fields.mp4 "A lovely road beside the green tea fields.")

I was wanting a coffee stop, and found a lovely spot with a lovely owner: Ichi Coffee Roastery. It's generally a bit of a hack when travelling: if you want good coffee, a Google Maps search for "coffee roastery" is usually pretty good. The owner's Instagram is just lovely vibes, and he really treats his customers well; give him [a follow](https://www.instagram.com/ichi_coffee_roastery/). Or just enjoy [the picture he took of me](https://www.instagram.com/p/DH-f98wTRQj/?img_index=1) when I visited.

![Neil standing indoors at Ichi Coffee Roastery in cycling kit, smiling at the camera, in a photograph taken by the roastery's owner](./_assets/neil-at-ichi-coffee-roastery.jpg "The picture he took of me.")

![A pour-over coffee setup with glass carafes and an orange mug of coffee on a wooden counter at Ichi Coffee Roastery](./_assets/pour-over-coffee-at-ichi-roastery.jpg "A lovely spot with a lovely owner.")

Now, castles. Everyone's seen them in the movies, but they really are everywhere in Japan, and some of them are lovely. Very different from European castles, being made of wood, but a lovely aesthetic.

![A white Japanese castle keep seen across a park lined with cherry blossom trees, with visitors walking below](./_assets/japanese-castle-across-blossom-lined-moat.jpg "The first of many castles.")

![The same castle keep seen from another angle, framed by cherry blossom trees under a cloudy sky](./_assets/castle-keep-among-cherry-blossom.jpg "The first of many castles.")

One thing you notice cycling in Japan for a while is just how quiet it is. I think it's because of some pretty aggressive town speed limits, generally around 30km/h. So there's just so little car noise, and it makes cycling on the road feel really safe: that's about the speed a bicycle can go anyway, with a bit of effort, so cars aren't much faster than you are.

![A quiet residential street with a marked cycle lane between houses, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/quiet-residential-street-cycle-lane.mp4 "So little car noise at all.")

![A quiet path running beside a grassy drainage canal, with a few houses in the distance](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/quiet-path-beside-drainage-canal.mp4 "So little car noise at all.")

After passing a factory that produces the best soft drink in the world (Pocari Sweat, apparently), I arrived at some excellent views to end the day.

![A large factory complex seen across open fields, identified in the ride notes as a Pocari Sweat drinks factory](./_assets/pocari-sweat-factory-across-fields.jpg "The best soft drink in the world.")

![A winding river between hills, seen from a bridge in warm evening light](./_assets/winding-river-view-from-bridge.jpg "Excellent views to end the day.")

![A loaded touring bike parked on a grassy bank overlooking a wide bay, with a red torii gate standing in the water](./_assets/touring-bike-overlooking-bay-with-torii.jpg "Excellent views to end the day.")

## Castles and quiet towns to Lake Biwa

The next day was pretty uneventful, I was starting to get fatigued by this point. I don't know if you noticed in the previous recordings, but it was a pretty constant headwind from the start! So it was building up for sure. One more day after this until a rest day, though.

![A straight road past village houses, filmed while riding into a headwind](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/straight-road-past-village-houses.mp4 "A pretty constant headwind from the start.")

Didn't stop me having a weird dessert and a Nagoya classic along the way.

![A green matcha cream roll dessert on a small plate with a fork, served on a café table](./_assets/matcha-cream-roll-dessert.jpg "A weird dessert.")

![A plate of Nagoya-style fried chicken wings alongside a beer, a classic Nagoya dish](./_assets/nagoya-style-fried-chicken-and-beer.jpg "A Nagoya classic.")

Finally the rest day was coming up! But I was treated to a lovely short ride first, through some fantastic roads. A pat on the back for the route planning! A thing I noticed on this trip was just how wonderful the view was while cruising over a bridge, I'm not showing all the videos of these in this post, but they always had me in awe.

![The view down to a river from a red steel truss bridge, filmed while riding across it](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/view-from-red-steel-truss-bridge.mp4 "They always had me in awe.")

But a bridge is a bit of a cheat code for a great view in Japan, sometimes it's just the road ahead that delights. I can never get enough of hills on quiet roads, or quiet roads with cherry blossoms.

![A straight farm road running toward distant hills under a blue sky, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/straight-farm-road-toward-hills.mp4 "The road ahead delights.")

![A cherry-blossom-lined farm road with hills closer in the background, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/cherry-lined-farm-road-near-hills.mp4 "The road ahead delights.")

![A farm road lined with mature cherry blossom trees in full bloom, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/farm-road-lined-with-cherry-blossom.mp4 "Quiet roads with cherry blossoms.")

To top it off, a rest day at a beautiful spot on the coast of Lake Biwa. It was actually really cheap to stay, with a nice small town and great food options. Hard to beat.

![A calm, wide view across Lake Biwa from a jetty, under an overcast sky](./_assets/calm-view-of-lake-biwa.jpg "A beautiful spot on the coast of Lake Biwa.")

![Lake Biwa seen through a hotel room window, with faint reflections visible in the glass](./_assets/lake-biwa-view-through-hotel-window.jpg "Actually really cheap to stay.")

The rest day took me on a short adventure to see yet another castle. This one had a few "samurai" firing guns, which was fun (and loud!) to see. Some lovely views, and the day ended with unagi: so damn tasty.

![A white castle keep on a hillside, framed by cherry blossom trees under a blue sky](./_assets/white-castle-keep-with-cherry-blossom.jpg "Yet another castle.")
