## An island before Shikoku

Now, it's time for a ferry: it isn't a good bikepacking trip unless there's a ferry involved.

![The frothy white wake of a ferry seen from its stern, with railings in the foreground](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/ferry-wake-view-from-stern.mp4 "A ferry involved.")

![A calm grey sea stretching to a distant hazy coastline, seen from a ferry deck at dusk](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/calm-sea-view-from-ferry-deck.mp4 "A ferry involved.")

These trips could always be designed as the straightest, easiest route possible, but where's the fun in that? So I added an "island hop" in the middle of the Japan leg, and rather than go around it on the coast road, I went straight up and over.

![A quiet road climbing gently between houses and a wooded hillside at the start of the island climb](./_assets/road-beginning-the-island-climb.jpg "Straight up and over.")

This was an incredible climb - not only did the roads snake around the hill in such a cool way while providing amazing views, but it was also so quiet and empty of cars. Not as if there'd be much traffic on a random island. One of my favourite climbs ever, with the cherry on top being that incredible view at the summit.

![A winding hillside road with a crash barrier, cherry blossom, and hills in the background](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/winding-island-climb-road-cherry-blossom.mp4 "One of my favourite climbs ever.")

![A winding hillside road curving past hazy layered hills](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/winding-island-climb-road-hazy-hills.mp4 "One of my favourite climbs ever.")

![A hillside road with a crash barrier climbing between trees](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/winding-island-climb-road-guardrail.mp4 "One of my favourite climbs ever.")

![A loaded touring bike leaning against a wooden fence, overlooking misty layered hills](./_assets/loaded-bike-at-island-climb-viewpoint.jpg "The cherry on top.")

![Misty, layered hills seen from the top of the island climb](./_assets/misty-layered-hills-from-island-summit.jpg "The cherry on top.")

What I loved, too, is that even though this is just a random island, the road surface is unbeatable. Really puts the UK to utter shame.

![A smooth road curving past cherry blossom with a town visible below](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/smooth-island-road-with-cherry-blossom.mp4 "The road surface is unbeatable.")

But of course, I had to leave the island again after this slight adventure, so back on another ferry.

![A ferry's suspension cables and car deck as it departs the island, with the town visible across the water](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/leaving-island-by-ferry.mp4 "Back on another ferry.")

## The mountain stage

On Shikoku! For sure my favourite of the main islands of Japan so far, only been to three after all.

![A flat, straight cycle path beside a river with pylons in the distance, riding onto Shikoku](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/flat-riverside-cycle-path-into-shikoku.mp4 "On Shikoku!")

The next day I had three routes planned: two into the mountains and one along the coast, the idea being "am I too tired?" after the island, or "is the weather too dangerous?" for the mountains. I was feeling fresh, so I wanted the hills, but the weather being pure fog was tempting me not to. But I'm on an "aventure"! And "aventures" have to have some danger. I decided the forest path would be too risky in the fog, after the earlier hike-a-bike had taught me how steep and slimy that kind of path can get wet, so the main road it was.

![A narrow road beside a traditional walled building, with hills hidden by fog in the background](./_assets/narrow-road-beside-traditional-building-foggy-hills.jpg "The last chance to take the forest path, but you can't even see the mountains. They're supposed to be right in front of me here!")

The main road worried me a little, as I thought it might be busy, but it was actually relatively empty. However, it did give silent hill vibes.

![A road with a blue-edged cycle lane disappearing into thick fog](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/road-vanishing-into-thick-fog.mp4 "Silent hill vibes.")

At one point I heard a real rumble coming from up the road, then two "eyes" appeared in the fog ahead. Turned out to be a truck. Super freaky.

As an example of how foggy it was: this is what I could see, and this is what I was supposed to see. Crazy.

![A loaded bike leaning against a railing above a valley shrouded in thick fog](./_assets/bike-at-railing-in-misty-valley.jpg "This is what I could see.")

![A hillside town shrouded in fog, seen from a high vantage point with a large building below](./_assets/foggy-hillside-town-from-above.jpg "What I could actually see.")

![A tourist information board illustrating the mountain view on a clear day, for comparison with the actual fog](./_assets/information-board-showing-clear-day-view.jpg "What I was supposed to see.")

Then the mega tunnel. 1.6 kilometres long and super narrow. I knew it was coming and, to be honest, wasn't even sure I could cycle through it. But better ask forgiveness than permission, sometimes...

![A yellow tunnel warning sign beside the entrance to a narrow mountain tunnel](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/entrance-to-narrow-mountain-tunnel.mp4 "Better ask forgiveness than permission.")

![The dim interior of a long, narrow tunnel, with a bright opening visible far ahead](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/cycling-through-dark-narrow-tunnel.mp4 "The mega tunnel.")

But it was worth it for the waterfall at the other end.

![The entrance to a mountain tunnel with a yellow warning sign, framed by spring blossom](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/mountain-tunnel-entrance-with-warning-sign-and-blossom.mp4 "Worth it for the waterfall.")

The amazing thing, too, was that on the other side of the tunnel all the fog was gone. I was super excited, knowing it promised some incredible views.

![A clear view of layered mountain ridges and puffy clouds after the fog lifted](./_assets/clear-mountain-view-after-fog-lifted.jpg "All the fog was gone.")

![A road curving past a yellow chevron warning sign, with a clear valley view beyond](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/clear-valley-road-with-chevron-sign.mp4 "All the fog was gone.")

Just cycling through this incredible valley, with barely any traffic and amazing roads. Hands down one of the best cycling days of my life.

![A road on an elevated causeway curving through a mountain valley under a clear sky](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/elevated-road-through-mountain-valley.mp4 "Hands down one of the best cycling days of my life.")

Even had ways to avoid the tunnels!

![A road junction with warning signs, showing a tunnel entrance off to one side that can be avoided](./_assets/road-junction-bypassing-tunnel.jpg "Even had ways to avoid the tunnels!")

I love it when you see an incredible view, appreciate it, and then go over to see it from another angle: below the bridge, on the bridge, seeing the dam, on the dam.

![Cherry blossom branches framing a view down into a green mountain valley](./_assets/cherry-blossom-branches-above-mountain-valley.jpg "Endless great views.")

![A road bridge with a guardrail winding through a bright green valley](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/road-bridge-winding-through-green-valley.mp4 "From another angle.")

![A road on an elevated viaduct curving through a mountain valley, with a pylon in view](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/elevated-viaduct-road-curving-through-valley.mp4 "From another angle.")

![A guardrail-lined road bending around a wooded hillside](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/guardrail-road-bend-on-wooded-hillside.mp4 "From another angle.")

Just endless great views, and so damn quiet with barely any traffic.

![Riding a barriered road beside a river with a distant bridge and misty hills](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/barriered-road-beside-river-misty-hills.mp4 "So damn quiet, so little traffic.")

![A quiet mountain road junction with a cycle route sign, valley views beyond](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/quiet-junction-with-cycle-route-sign.mp4 "So damn quiet, so little traffic.")

My only issue was a bit of a lack of supplies, but the views were so good it was fine. Again, I'd prepared for this and had bought some Calorie Mate bars (Metal Gear Solid reference there) to get me through.

_But who cares about food when you have views like this_

![A calm river reflecting the sky, with a road running alongside it through the valley](./_assets/still-river-reflecting-sky-in-valley.jpg "But who cares about food when you have views like this.")

![A mountain road bending past a warning sign, with peaks in the distance](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/mountain-road-bend-beside-warning-sign.mp4 "But who cares about food when you have views like this.")

Well, you start to remember how hungry you are after you have to go through another 1km+ tunnel 😅. Well if every tunnel brought you to a mystical world like this, then I'll go through whatever tunnel you want.

![A hillside road with a guardrail, hazy mountain peaks, and power lines beyond](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/hillside-road-after-tunnel-with-hazy-peaks.mp4 "...then I'll go through whatever tunnel you want.")

![A sunlit mountain road with strong lens flare and layered peaks beyond](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/sunlit-mountain-road-with-lens-flare.mp4 "Whatever tunnel you want.")

The rest of the day was coming back out of the valley, down some more incredible roads. I was actually kind of sad to leave that amazing route behind. I'll have to return. I'm glad I took some last looks before it was out of sight.

![A road with a guardrail descending through layered green hills](./_assets/descending-road-through-layered-green-hills.jpg "Sad to leave that amazing route behind.")

![A winding road along a hazy, forested mountainside](./_assets/winding-road-along-hazy-mountainside.jpg "Coming back out of the valley.")

All that was left was to roll into town for another rest day. What a way to end what will likely be an unbeatable cycling day for the rest of my life.

![A quiet riverside cycle path glowing pink and orange at dusk, filmed while riding into town](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/dusk-riverside-road-into-town.mp4 "Rolling into town for another rest day.")
