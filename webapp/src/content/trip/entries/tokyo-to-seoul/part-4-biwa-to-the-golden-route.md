## A lap of Lake Biwa

Back on the bike for some of the best cycling infrastructure in Japan: there's a full cycle lane that goes all the way around Lake Biwa, so of course I took advantage. The whole way round you're cruising in the lane, away from cars, with an incredible view of the water. Highly recommend to everyone.

![A dedicated blue-marked cycle lane running beside Lake Biwa, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/dedicated-cycle-lane-beside-lake-biwa.mp4 "Away from the cars, with an incredible view.")

![A wide, calm view across Lake Biwa toward hills on the far shore](./_assets/wide-view-across-lake-biwa.jpg "Highly recommend to everyone.")

Planning the route, I'd noticed a bit of a viewpoint, but it looped back on itself, which I generally try to avoid. It looked like such a nice road and view that I made an exception. Also, it meant I could lighten the load of the bike, too. I found a quiet wall on the side of the road and hid one of the bags there:

![A cycle path running along a low stone wall on a hillside above Lake Biwa](./_assets/cycle-path-above-lake-biwa-hillside.jpg "A quiet wall to hide a bag behind.")

So I wouldn't have to slog it up the climb. Worked out well.

![A climbing road lined with cherry blossom trees and a stone retaining wall, filmed while riding without the bike's bags](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/climbing-road-lined-with-cherry-blossom.mp4 "Worked out well.")

![A bike with reduced luggage resting at a railing, overlooking Lake Biwa from a high viewpoint](./_assets/unloaded-bike-viewpoint-over-lake-biwa.jpg "Lighter, for the climb.")

![A winding descent road through a wooded hillside, filmed while riding back down](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/winding-descent-through-wooded-hillside.mp4 "Worked out well.")

![A panoramic view over Lake Biwa and its small islands from the hillside viewpoint](./_assets/panoramic-view-over-lake-biwa-islands.jpg "Worked out well.")

Back on the road, and you're just treated to truly endless great views of the lake, all from a great cycle lane. Honestly, one of the best cycling days of my life.

![A dedicated cycle lane beside Lake Biwa passing a row of parked cars, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/cycle-lane-beside-lake-with-parked-cars.mp4 "Endless great views.")

![A coastal road beside Lake Biwa with a guardrail and hillside, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/coastal-road-beside-lake-biwa.mp4 "Also just cool sights out on the water.")

![A small torii gate standing in the water near the shore of Lake Biwa](./_assets/small-torii-gate-in-lake-biwa.jpg "Cool sights out on the water, too.")

![A wooden shrine building standing on stilts out in Lake Biwa, known locally as the Ukimido](./_assets/shrine-on-stilts-in-lake-biwa.jpg "Cool sights out on the water, too.")

![A lakeside road with mountains visible in the distance, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/lakeside-road-with-mountain-view.mp4 "Not just lake, but mountains too.")

That evening, now so close to Kyoto, I jumped on a local train and paid a visit. I did see this pretty cool shot on the way there - a salaryman under the beauty of a cherry blossom tree.

![A man in a suit walking along a street beneath cherry blossom trees overhanging a stone wall](./_assets/man-in-suit-walking-beneath-cherry-blossom.jpg "A salaryman under the beauty of a cherry blossom tree.")

Kyoto itself was alright, but it really is super overcrowded with tourists (myself included this evening!), which kind of ruins the vibe. I'd been before, so I wasn't overly sad about it.

![A traditional building lit up at night, reflected in a still canal in Kyoto](./_assets/kyoto-canal-reflection-at-night.jpg "Kyoto, overcrowded but still lovely.")

![A traditional five-storey pagoda illuminated at night among Kyoto's older buildings](./_assets/illuminated-pagoda-at-night-in-kyoto.jpg "Kyoto, overcrowded but still lovely.")

## Kyoto, Osaka, and Himeji

The next day was perhaps one of the toughest of the trip: big miles, and an even bigger headwind. But it's Japan, so the toughest day is still a joy in itself. It was cutting between the two big popular cities of Kyoto and Osaka.

It started with some great cycle paths and another classic river-and-cherry-blossom view.

![A dedicated cycle path beside a canal with a mountain in the background, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/cycle-path-beside-canal-with-mountain.mp4 "It started with some great cycle paths.")

![A straight path between fields with yellow flowering crops and houses beyond, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/path-between-fields-of-yellow-flowers.mp4 "It started with some great cycle paths.")

![A canal path lined with blossoming cherry trees, with two people walking along it](./_assets/canal-path-lined-with-cherry-blossom.jpg "Another classic river-and-cherry-blossom view.")

My videos capture some really peaceful roads, but they didn't capture the really busy roads in the towns, with constant stop-start at the lights - this, with the strong headwind, was really sapping my strength.

![A narrow paved road curving beside a tall concrete retaining wall, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/narrow-road-beside-concrete-retaining-wall.mp4 "My videos capture some really peaceful roads.")

![A flat farmland road beside a drainage channel, filmed while riding through open fields](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/flat-farmland-road-beside-drainage-channel.mp4 "My videos capture some really peaceful roads.")

But even with all that, it's still Japan, so once I reached the peaceful areas past the towns, while still super tired, it was just beautiful.

![A straight rural road under an overcast sky, filmed while riding past distant houses](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/straight-rural-road-under-overcast-sky.mp4 "Still beautiful, even tired.")

![A rural road running between village houses, with hills visible under a grey sky](./_assets/rural-road-between-village-houses.jpg "Still beautiful, even tired.")

![A loaded touring bike propped against a railing beside a river, with hills in the background](./_assets/touring-bike-beside-river-and-hills.jpg "Still beautiful, even tired.")

![Cycling across a road bridge alongside passing traffic, with hills in the distance](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/cycling-across-road-bridge-with-traffic.mp4 "Still beautiful, even tired.")

There's always another castle to visit, too.

![A white castle keep illuminated against the night sky, with cherry blossom silhouettes in the foreground](./_assets/illuminated-castle-keep-at-night.jpg "Always another castle to visit.")

A rest day, so well timed after that difficult day. Being in Himeji, I had to go see what's regarded as the best castle in Japan (Himeji Castle), and it certainly didn't disappoint, even if it was a bit busy, as expected.

![Himeji Castle's large white keep rising above cherry blossom trees under a blue sky](./_assets/himeji-castle-with-cherry-blossom.jpg "The best castle in Japan.")

![The approach to Himeji Castle lined with red foliage, with visitors walking toward the keep](./_assets/himeji-castle-approach-with-red-foliage.jpg "Certainly didn't disappoint.")

![A high view over Himeji's rooftops and surrounding hills, seen from the castle](./_assets/view-over-himeji-city-from-castle.jpg "Certainly didn't disappoint.")

I then spent the evening walking around at a gentle pace, enjoying the peace of Japanese cities, it really is incredible, even with all the cars.

![A tall stone monument with cherry blossoms in the foreground, under a clear blue sky](./_assets/tall-stone-monument-with-cherry-blossoms.jpg "Enjoying the peace of Japanese cities.")

I sat up here for about an hour, just watching the trains go by and the sunset slowly come on.

![A sunset over a hillside town, with silhouetted buildings and trees in the foreground and the sun low on the horizon](./_assets/sunset-over-himeji-from-hilltop.jpg "Watching the sunset slowly come on.")
