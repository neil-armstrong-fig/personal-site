## Escaping Tokyo

I'd done a bit of reading before the trip, and there are three general ways you cycle in Japan. On the road, you hug the left side, never the middle. Drivers were always great at overtaking, and always, _always_ indicated, I did try to find someone not doing it, and never did. On a rare cycle lane, when you got one, I honestly thought there'd be more of them. And on a footpath: totally legal, with speed restrictions, though everyone flows pretty well.

![A road with a red-surfaced dedicated cycle lane running alongside it, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/first-dedicated-cycle-lane-in-japan.mp4 "One of the rarer dedicated cycle lanes.")

Lunch was always pretty easy to find, if not at a café or restaurant, then at a convenience store. I did manage to get the famous Japanese-style pancakes pretty early on, though.

![A stack of fluffy Japanese-style pancakes with syrup, served alongside a coffee on a café table](./_assets/japanese-souffle-pancakes-with-coffee.jpg "The famous Japanese-style pancakes.")

![A Japanese convenience store storefront with a car and a bicycle parked outside](./_assets/convenience-store-with-parked-bike-and-car.jpg "An extremely common stop, the conbini.")

I had some spots planned for the first day, but some just happened as I rolled by, cherry blossoms and all.

![A quiet rural road running beside an open field, with a car parked in the distance](./_assets/rural-road-beside-a-paddy-field.jpg "Some spots planned, some just happened.")

![Cherry blossom trees in bloom lining both banks of a river path, with a few people walking below](./_assets/cherry-blossoms-lining-a-riverside-path.jpg "Some spots just happened as I rolled by.")

![Riding beside a row of blossoming cherry trees along a fenced cycle path](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/riding-past-blossoming-cherry-trees.mp4 "Cherry blossoms and all.")

Then I got just first sight of some hills!

![A rural road with power lines leading toward low hills on the horizon](./_assets/first-hills-appearing-on-the-horizon.jpg "First sight of some hills.")

Then I had my first real panic of the trip 😅. I'd added a detour up a few steps to get a nice view. The view was nice! Did have to climb a few steps, as you can see my bike here.

![A view over a Japanese hillside town and coastline from a cherry-blossom-framed viewpoint](./_assets/hillside-viewpoint-over-japanese-town.jpg "The view, from the top of the steps.")

![A view over a hillside town's rooftops from partway up a flight of steps](./_assets/view-over-rooftops-from-steps-detour.jpg "Did have to climb a few steps.")

So I enjoyed it for a bit, climbed back down, and got on my way. All the way down to the main road, I slapped my leg just to check my phone was there. It wasn't. _Where is my phone? Did it fall out? I can use my spare phone to find it, maybe, but no internet, no wifi... It must be back up that hill, no way it fell out of my pocket._ Yep, left it there. Such relief, though. That was the last time I left my phone anywhere. Lesson learnt 😅

![A wooden bench at a viewpoint overlooking a coastal town, where a phone was left behind and recovered](./_assets/phone-recovered-on-park-bench.jpg "Right where I'd left it.")

It's all good, though, treated to some tasty ramen at the end of the day to make up for it. Japanese ramen certainly does hit.

![A bowl of ramen topped with nori and a boiled egg, beside an iced drink with a straw](./_assets/ramen-bowl-with-iced-drink.jpg "Japanese ramen certainly does hit.")

## The first hunt for Fuji

The next day started with a bit of rain, so I decided to hide indoors and enjoy a coffee while it passed.

![Empty outdoor café tables and chairs on a wet, rainy morning](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/sheltering-for-coffee-in-the-rain.mp4 "Hiding indoors for a coffee.")

It didn't quite pass by the time I started, but I needed to get going if I wanted to finish in daylight. I'll take a bit of light rain (I'm Irish, I'm not going to melt).

![A waterfall dropping beside a red footbridge next to the road, filmed in the rain](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/waterfall-beside-the-road-in-the-rain.mp4 "A bit of light rain.")

I had a bit of a climb ahead, after all.

![A road and stream running through a valley toward hills at the base of a climb](./_assets/valley-road-at-base-of-the-climb.jpg "A bit of a climb ahead.")

As I started the climb I was treated to my first of many tunnels. These are never a lot of fun, I'll be honest 😅. Thankfully this one was pretty short, to break me in easy, there are much longer ones to come. They can get very cold, cars sound extremely loud and scary, and it's difficult to see with sunglasses on (though that wasn't an issue this trip, as I'd forgotten my prescription sunglasses 😂).

![A cycling computer screen showing the elevation profile and remaining ascent of the climb, with power and cadence at zero](./_assets/bike-computer-showing-climb-profile.jpg "It's funny looking at this graph now, it scared me at the time, but after all my trips since, it just looks like a nice steady effort 😂")

![A road leading into a tunnel entrance in a hillside, seen while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/entering-the-first-road-tunnel.mp4 "Breaking me in easy.")

After a bit of climbing I finally got to the top.

![Bare trees along the roadside on a foggy hairpin climb](./_assets/bare-trees-beside-hairpin-climb.jpg "A bit of climbing to finally get to the top.")

![A loaded touring bike parked at the base of a set of steps leading up to a viewpoint, wet from rain](./_assets/bike-left-at-base-of-summit-steps.jpg "Finally got to the top.")

I'd purposefully planned this climb, as you can get an amazing view of Mount Fuji from up there. And what a view it was...

![A bike leaning against a railing at a viewpoint, overlooking a valley completely obscured by cloud where Mount Fuji should be](./_assets/viewpoint-bench-with-cloud-covered-view.jpg "Yep. All clouds.")

Yep, all clouds. You win some, you lose some. Enjoy this accurate reproduction instead:

![The same cloud-covered viewpoint, with a green line doodled over the photo sketching where Mount Fuji would be](./_assets/fuji-outline-doodled-over-cloudy-view.jpg "An accurate reproduction.")

But it's all good. After a pretty careful descent on the wet roads, I arrived and, of course, treated myself to more tasty ramen.

![A bowl of ramen with a fried egg, served alongside a small rice dish and drinks on a restaurant table](./_assets/bowl-of-ramen-with-side-dishes.jpg "More tasty ramen.")

## Fuji, seen

Next day I was determined. It was a clear day, surely I'd see Fuji today! The morning café came close, so close.

![A café car park with several cars parked and hills visible in the distance, Mount Fuji still hidden by cloud](./_assets/car-park-with-clouds-hiding-fuji.jpg "So close.")

My planning had spotted a nice hill for a possible view, and since nobody likes backtracking on a route, I'd planned one way up and a different road down. The problem was, the way up turned into a bit of a forest path. Hike-a-bike it is.

![A narrow forest path climbing steeply through trees, pushing the bike rather than riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/pushing-the-bike-up-a-forest-path.mp4 "Hike-a-bike it is.")

![A wide view over a coastal town and hills from the top of the forest hike-a-bike climb](./_assets/view-from-top-of-forest-hike-a-bike.jpg "Great view, though.")

Then, after cycling up an absolutely lovely hill road running parallel to the main road:

![A quiet hill road with sea views running parallel to the main coast road below](./_assets/hill-road-running-parallel-to-coast-road.jpg "A lovely hill road running parallel to the main road.")

I was just taking a nice photo of the bike at the top, and I saw it. Just peeking its head!

![A loaded touring bike beside a guardrail, with Mount Fuji's snow-capped peak just visible over distant hills](./_assets/mount-fuji-first-glimpsed-over-hills.jpg "Just peeking its head!")

![A coastal viewpoint with hills and sea, Mount Fuji's peak visible faintly in the distance, filmed while riding](/trips/tokyo-to-seoul/coastal-view-with-fuji-peeking-through.mp4 "Just peeking its head!")

![Mount Fuji's snow-capped peak rising above the hills along the coastline](./_assets/mount-fuji-visible-above-the-coastline.jpg "Fuji, properly seen at last.")

Continuing on, I reached a road I'd planned as there wasn't really much of an alternative: a quiet secondary road along the coast, a main road, or a big mountain pass. Obviously I took the coastal road. There were lots of warning signs, all in Japanese, but the road wasn't closed, so I thought, _maybe just some roadworks I can walk around_. It was totally empty, in a creepy way. Not a soul about. Then, about halfway down, I found a completely closed tunnel. No entry. Like a zombie movie.

![A closed tunnel entrance blocked by barriers and warning signs on an empty road](./_assets/closed-tunnel-entrance-with-no-entry-barriers.jpg "Like a zombie movie.")

So I checked the phone: is there another way I missed? Nope, still just the main road or the mountains. So I made the call to get the train to bypass it - not ideal, as I like cycling the whole thing, but there are no rules, really. Back into the bag it went, for a very short train journey.

![The bike packed into its rinko bag, waiting on a Japanese train platform beside route maps](./_assets/bike-bagged-on-train-platform.jpg "Back into the bag, for one stop.")
